# lawyer — correspondence, mostly. Mends nothing, threatens nothing, and
# what it opens stays open for years. See
# ../new-class-prompt.txt for the grammar and the fairness rules.

type: lawyer
desc: Cease and desist. Drains the will to live, billably.

[damage]
minor    | {attacker} adds {target} to a thread for {damage} damage.
minor    | {attacker} asks {target} one question and writes the answer down — {damage} damage.
minor    | {attacker} copies in two more people {target} has not met — {damage} damage.
minor    | {attacker} serves {target} in front of everyone, politely — {damage} damage.
minor    | {attacker} sends {target} a letter that is technically an offer to help — {damage} damage.

medium   | {attacker} requests everything {target} has written since March — {damage} damage.
medium   | {attacker} enters {target}'s own words into the record for {damage} damage.
medium   | {attacker} moves the date, and {target} rearranges a month around it — {damage} damage.
medium   | {attacker} sends {target} eleven pages clarifying one sentence — {damage} damage.
medium   | {attacker} bills the time spent explaining the bill to {target} — {damage} damage.

major    | {attacker} files, and {target} discovers what disclosure means — {damage} damage.
major    | {attacker} takes {target} through the same afternoon for a fourth time — {damage} damage.
major    | {attacker} produces the thing {target} assumed had been deleted — {damage} damage.
major    | {attacker} explains to {target} what the retainer has been funding — {damage} damage.

critical | {attacker} moves to dismiss {target}. {damage} CRITICAL damage!
critical | {attacker} wins on a point {target} conceded in the first week. {damage} CRITICAL damage!
critical | {attacker} sends {target} the final invoice, itemised. {damage} CRITICAL damage!
critical | {attacker} is in no hurry at all, and {target} cannot afford one. {damage} CRITICAL damage!

[dot]
curse | protracted suit | {attacker} files a {affliction} against {target} and settles in.
curse | standing action | {attacker} opens a {affliction} on {target} with no end date.
curse | counterclaim    | {attacker} attaches a {affliction} to {target} and waits.
curse | open matter     | {attacker} leaves the {affliction} on {target} indefinitely.
drain | billable hour   | {attacker} starts a {affliction} against {target} and forgets it.
drain | running tab     | {attacker} opens a {affliction} on {target} that does not close.
drain | hourly rate     | {attacker} applies a {affliction} to {target} and lets it run.
drain | cost order      | {attacker} hangs a {affliction} on {target} and moves on.
rot   | endless motion  | {attacker} lodges the {affliction} against {target} and waits.
rot   | sealed exhibit  | {attacker} enters a {affliction} concerning {target}.
rot   | long docket     | {attacker} puts {target} on a {affliction} and closes the folder.
rot   | further filing  | {attacker} promises {target} a {affliction} and delivers it.

[death]
{attacker} closes the file and sends the last invoice to {target}'s estate.
{attacker} wins, eventually, and {target} is not present for it.
{attacker} marks the matter concluded and does not think about {target}.

[decay]
curse | The {affliction} {attacker} filed against {target} enters month four — {damage} damage.
curse | {target} answers {attacker}'s {affliction} again for {damage} damage.
drain | The {affliction} {attacker} started against {target} runs on — {damage} damage.
drain | {target} opens {attacker}'s {affliction} and loses {damage} damage.
rot   | The {affliction} {attacker} lodged concerning {target} costs {damage} damage.
rot   | {target} reads {attacker}'s {affliction} to the end — {damage} damage.

[decay-kill]
curse | The {affliction} {attacker} filed outlives {target} and continues.
drain | {target} settles. {attacker}'s {affliction} carries on without them.
rot   | {target} does not reach the end of {attacker}'s {affliction}.
