# shaman — drums, rattles, dances and a heavy staff. Most of what it
# does arrives late; some of it puts people back on their feet. See
# ../new-class-prompt.txt for the grammar and the fairness rules.

type: shaman
desc: Drums, rattles and a heavy staff. Slow harm, some mending.

[damage]
minor    | {attacker} raps {target} across the shins with the staff for {damage} damage.
minor    | {attacker} shakes the rattle once and {target}'s teeth pick up the buzz — {damage} damage.
minor    | {attacker} scatters hot ash across {target}'s eyes for {damage} damage.
minor    | {attacker} stamps once and the ground nips at {target}'s ankles — {damage} damage.

medium   | {attacker} swings the staff into {target}'s ribs and lets the wood ring — {damage} damage.
medium   | {attacker} stamps twice and the floor bucks {target} off their feet for {damage} damage.
medium   | {attacker} beats the drum until {target} bleeds from the nose — {damage} damage.
medium   | {attacker} calls a gust through the room that sands {target}'s face raw — {damage} damage.

major    | {attacker} brings the staff down on {target}'s shoulder and hears it crack — {damage} damage.
major    | {attacker} calls the wind in low and it takes {target} off their feet for {damage} damage.
major    | {attacker} dances the circle tighter around {target} until something inside tears — {damage} damage.
major    | {attacker} spins the staff full-circle and catches {target} across both knees — {damage} damage.

critical | {attacker} beats the drum until {target}'s heart tries to keep the rhythm. {damage} CRITICAL damage!
critical | {attacker} brings the staff down across {target}'s spine. {damage} CRITICAL damage!
critical | {attacker} calls every ancestor at once and {target} comes apart under the answer. {damage} CRITICAL damage!
critical | {attacker} asks the ground to open under {target} and close again after. {damage} CRITICAL damage!

[dot]
curse | binding word  | {attacker} names {target} aloud and the {affliction} settles over them to stay.
curse | black mark    | {attacker} paints a {affliction} on {target} that will not wipe off.
curse | old debt      | {attacker} calls in something older than {target}, and the {affliction} stays to collect.
curse | hollow name   | {attacker} takes {target}'s name away and leaves the {affliction} sitting where it was.
rot   | creeping rot  | {attacker} presses wet earth into {target}'s wound and the {affliction} takes root.
rot   | grave mould   | {attacker} breathes {affliction} across {target} and lets it settle in the warm places.
rot   | root blight   | {attacker} pushes something living into {target} and the {affliction} spreads on its own.
chill | deep chill    | {attacker} lets the cold off the drumskin into {target} — a {affliction} that will not lift.
chill | winter breath | {attacker} exhales across {target} and leaves {affliction} sitting in the lungs.
chill | grey frost    | {attacker} touches {target}'s wrist and the {affliction} starts climbing from the fingers in.

[heal]
minor | {attacker} packs {target}'s wound with bitter leaf and ties it shut — {heal} hit points.
minor | {attacker} beats a slow rhythm over {target} until the breathing falls in with it. {heal} hit points.
minor | {attacker} spits smoke across {target}'s cut and scolds it closed — {heal} hit points.
major | {attacker} dances the whole circle over {target} and hauls them back to their feet — {heal} hit points.
major | {attacker} calls something old to look at {target}, and it decides to mend them. {heal} hit points.
major | {attacker} lends {target} the drum's own heartbeat until theirs picks the rhythm back up. {heal} hit points.

[death]
{attacker} stops drumming. {target} stops with it.
{attacker} steps over {target} and scatters ash across the face.
{attacker} lets the circle close over {target} and does not open it again.

[decay]
curse | The {affliction} {attacker} laid on {target} comes due for {damage} damage.
curse | {target} hears their own name in {attacker}'s voice again — the {affliction} takes {damage} damage.
rot   | The {affliction} {attacker} planted spreads a hand's width under {target}'s skin — {damage} damage.
rot   | {attacker} is nowhere near, and the {affliction} eats {damage} damage deeper into {target}.
chill | The {affliction} {attacker} left climbs one knuckle further up {target} — {damage} damage.
chill | {target} cannot get warm; the {affliction} {attacker} left takes another {damage} damage.

[decay-kill]
curse | The {affliction} {attacker} spoke over {target} collects the last of it.
rot   | The {affliction} {attacker} planted finally uses {target} up.
chill | The {affliction} {attacker} left reaches {target}'s heart and settles in.
