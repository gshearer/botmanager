# paladin — shield and sworn word at close range. Mends a little, and
# what it swears over a body stays sworn. See
# ../new-class-prompt.txt for the grammar and the fairness rules.

minor | {attacker} sets a hand on {target}'s breastplate until the shaking stops — {heal} hit points.

[death]

{attacker} finishes {target} and keeps the promise anyway.
{attacker} says the last of the oath over {target} and means it.

[decay]
curse | The {affliction} {attacker} swore over {target} comes due for {damage} damage.
curse | {target} cannot put {attacker}'s {affliction} down; it takes {damage} damage.
curse | {attacker} is elsewhere, and the {affliction} holds {target} to {damage} damage.

[decay-kill]
curse | The {affliction} {attacker} swore over {target} collects the rest.
curse | {target} is held to {attacker}'s {affliction} until nothing is left to hold.
