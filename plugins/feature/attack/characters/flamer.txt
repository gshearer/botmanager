# flamer — correspondence at volume, sustained past all reason. Mends
# nothing, and the thread goes on without anybody tending it. See
# ../new-class-prompt.txt for the grammar and the fairness rules.

type: flamer
desc: All caps Usenet rants. Lingering, inescapable flame wars.
